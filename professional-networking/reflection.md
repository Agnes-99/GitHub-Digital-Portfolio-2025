# Professional Networking: Reflection (STAR)

## Situation
As part of my Work Readiness training, I completed the **Professional Networking** course. This module showed me how crucial it is to build strong and meaningful professional relationships that can support growth, learning, and career opportunities, especially in the tech industry.

## Task
My goal was to develop practical networking skills that I can use both online and in-person. I wanted to learn how to connect with professionals in my field, create a lasting impression and establish a network that could help me grow as a software developer.

## Action
I completed all the lessons and quizzes with 100% accuracy. I learned:
- The **primary purpose of networking** is to build mutually valuable professional relationships.  
- How to follow the *golden rule of networking*: give value first before expecting anything in return.  
- How to prepare for networking events, including crafting a confident personal pitch and practising clear introductions.  
- Common mistakes to avoid, such as being pushy or not following up.  
- How to maintain professionalism on platforms like LinkedIn while engaging with other professionals.  

I applied these skills by reaching out to developers, mentors and recruiters online and by practising my introduction and pitch during mock networking exercises.

## Result
This module taught me that networking is more than just exchanging contacts, it’s about building long term relationships that can lead to mentorship, collaborations and career opportunities. Since completing the module, I’ve grown my LinkedIn network, gained more confidence in approaching professionals and already started receiving helpful guidance and opportunities from my connections.

## Future Use
I plan to continue building my professional network by attending tech events, engaging in online communities and connecting with people who inspire growth. These networking skills will help me advance as a developer and stay visible and relevant in my industry.
