# Business Communication: Reflection (STAR)

## Situation
As part of our Work Readiness training, I did the **Business Communication** course. It taught me how to communicate in a professional way, whether it’s writing emails, talking in meetings or even showing up properly on virtual calls. I realised that good communication really affects how people see you at work.

## Task
My goal was to understand what professional communication looks like and how to use it in real situations. I wanted to learn how to write better emails, speak clearly and keep a respectful tone when talking to others, especially in a work or internship setting.

## Action
I went through all the lessons and quizzes in the module. I learnt:
- The main purpose of business communication is to **share information clearly and respectfully**.  
- **Non-verbal communication** (like body language and facial expressions) also says a lot about you.  
- Use **formal language** in professional settings to show respect.  
- Keep your tone **polite and professional** in emails.  
- Always keep your **virtual background neat** when joining online meetings.  

I also started applying these lessons in my internship and in group work at university, like writing better emails and communicating more clearly with my teammates.

## Result
After completing the module, I got **100%** and felt more confident in how I express myself. I now pay attention to how I sound and present myself, whether in person or online. These small improvements have made a big difference in how I interact with people in a professional space.
