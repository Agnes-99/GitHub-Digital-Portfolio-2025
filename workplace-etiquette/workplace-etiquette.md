# Workplace Etiquette: Reflection (STAR)

## Situation
As part of my Work Readiness training, I completed the **Workplace Etiquette** course. This module taught me the rules and behaviours that help me act professionally, respect colleagues, and maintain a positive work environment.

## Task
My goal was to understand how to handle different workplace situations, such as managing emails, attending meetings on time, handling confidential information, and using personal devices appropriately, so that I can present myself as a professional employee.

## Action
I completed all lessons and quizzes with **100% completion**. I learned:
- How to respond to rude or offensive emails calmly and professionally.  
- The importance of punctuality, like arriving before a scheduled meeting starts.  
- How to handle confidential information safely and responsibly.  
- When it’s acceptable to use personal phones at work and when it isn’t.  
- Proper procedures for leaving work early for personal reasons.  

I also practised applying these rules in my daily life during group work, virtual meeting, and while communicating with lecturers and teammates, making sure I maintained professionalism at all times.

## Result
After completing this module, I feel confident in my ability to behave professionally in any workplace. I understand that small actions, like being on time, respecting confidentiality, and communicating politely, have a big impact on how colleagues and supervisors perceive me. Applying these skills has already improved my teamwork and professionalism in class projects and internship settings.

## Future Use
I plan to continue following workplace etiquette in every professional setting, both online and in-person. This will help me maintain a strong reputation, work well with teams, and advance in my career as a software developer.
