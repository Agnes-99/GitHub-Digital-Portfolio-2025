# Mock Interview — Reflection (STAR)

## Situation
As part of my Work Readiness training, I participated in a **Mock Interview** exercise. This activity simulated a real job interview, allowing me to practise answering questions, presenting myself professionally, and applying the skills I learned in the Interview Skills module.

## Task
My goal was to gain confidence in a realistic interview setting, test my communication and technical explanation skills, and identify areas I could improve before attending actual interviews or internship assessments.

## Action
During the mock interview, I:
- Practised answering common interview questions using the **CAR (Context, Action, Result) method**.  
- Prepared a concise and confident **personal pitch** about my skills, projects, and career goals.  
- Focused on **professional body language**, eye contact, and tone of voice to create a strong impression.  
- Received feedback from my peers on clarity, confidence and delivery.  
- Reflected immediately on what I could improve and revised my answers for future interviews.

I also recorded parts of the mock interview, which helped me see how I appeared to an interviewer and adjust my presentation accordingly.

## Result
The mock interview gave me a realistic experience of the pressures and expectations of a real interview. I gained confidence in answering questions clearly, structuring my responses with examples, and presenting myself as a professional. The feedback I received helped me identify areas for growth, like elaborating on technical projects and maintaining composure under unexpected questions.

## Future Use
I will continue practising mock interviews regularly, using feedback to improve my answers and presentation. This exercise has prepared me to approach real interviews with confidence, professionalism, and the ability to communicate my skills effectively as a developer.
