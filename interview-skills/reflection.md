# Interview Skills: Reflection (STAR)

### Module Overview
The **Interview Skills** course was one of the most valuable parts of my Work Readiness training. It gave me the confidence and structure I needed to perform well in real interviews. I learnt how to prepare before the interview, communicate clearly, and present myself professionally, both in how I speak and how I carry myself.

---

## Situation
Before doing this module, interviews used to make me nervous because I never knew what to expect or how to answer tricky questions. During the Work Readiness programme, we were introduced to the **Interview Skills** course to help us understand how interviews work and how to express our skills confidently.

---

## Task
My goal was to improve my communication and confidence when talking about myself in interviews. I wanted to learn how to answer questions properly, use professional body language and make a good first impression that reflects both my personality and my technical background as an aspiring developer.

---

## Action
I completed the entire course and all quizzes with **100% accuracy**. Through this module, I learnt:
- The **main purpose of an interview** is for the employer to understand who you are, how you think and how you fit into the company.  
- The **CAR method (Context, Action, Result)** helps you structure your answers and gives real life examples that show your experience and growth.  
- **Researching the company** before an interview helps you speak with confidence and ask thoughtful questions.  
- The best way to answer **“Tell me about yourself”** is to focus on your education, achievements, skills, and career goals, not your personal life.  
- When asked about your **greatest weakness**, it’s best to be honest but show how you’re improving on it.  

I also practiced answering common questions out loud and recorded myself to see how I sounded. I realised that the way you speak, sit, and smile also says a lot, it’s not just about what you say, but how you say it.  

During my internship interview, I used what I learned, I researched the company beforehand, prepared examples using the CAR method, and kept my tone professional but friendly. It made a huge difference in how confidently I responded.

---

## Result
By the end of the module, I felt fully prepared and much more confident in my interview skills. I understood how to take control of the conversation by focusing on my strengths and aligning them with what the company needs.  

This course taught me that **interviews are a two-way conversation**, not just a test. It’s about showing that you can bring value, communicate clearly, and handle pressure professionally.  

Because of this, I performed really well in my internship interview and I could see that my preparation made me stand out. I now approach every interview as a learning experience rather than something to fear.

---

## Future Use
Going forward, I plan to use these skills in every interview I attend. As a developer, I often have to explain my projects or technical ideas and I’ll keep applying the CAR method to communicate in a clear and structured way.  
I also plan to keep improving my confidence by doing more mock interviews and practicing with classmates and mentors.  

The Interview Skills module didn’t just teach me how to get through an interview, it taught me how to **present myself as a professional**, ready for any opportunity.
